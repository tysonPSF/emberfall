# Sound effect sources

Every recording here is public domain (CC0 1.0), trimmed, layered, pitched and
leveled by `tools/audio/sfx.py`, which downloads the packs itself:

- **Impact Sounds** and **RPG Audio** by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), CC0 1.0
- **80 CC0 RPG SFX** and **80 CC0 creature SFX** by rubberduck (opengameart.org), CC0 1.0

The weapon swooshes, the bowstring, the chimes, the cast hum and the level-up
fanfare are synthesized by the same script.
